# Seedance 2.5 成功 Prompt 範例庫

這份檔案只放「可複用結構」與經實戰確認有效的 Prompt 寫法。
不要把一次性 Prompt 當永久規則。

## 範例 1：快速抽出道具
### 適用
角色從後腰、口袋、腰間快速拿出道具。

### Prompt
Medium shot, eye-level camera.  
The character quickly reaches one hand behind the lower back, then in one clear and forceful motion pulls the object out and raises it fully into frame.  
The movement is fast, obvious, and large enough to read clearly.  
The upper body reacts naturally to the motion.  
Facial expression remains consistent with the reference image.  
Preserve the exact face, hairstyle, hair length, outfit, accessories, color scheme, and body proportions.  
Do not redesign the character.  
Do not add extra props.  
Do not change the object into another item.

### 為什麼有效
- 指定起始位置
- 指定動作順序
- 指定速度
- 指定幅度
- 指定最後狀態

## 範例 2：角色嫌棄反應
### Prompt
Medium close-up, eye-level camera, subtle slow push-in.  
The character initially looks confused, then gradually shifts into a visibly disgusted and skeptical expression.  
The eyebrows tighten slightly, the eyes narrow, and the mouth shows restrained annoyance.  
Keep the body mostly still so the facial expression remains the focus.  
Preserve the exact face, hairstyle, outfit, accessories, and body proportions.

### 適合
- 角色反應鏡頭
- 社死場面
- 吐槽鏡頭
- 對白後反應

---

## 範例 3：眼神由無光到恢復
### Prompt
Close-up shot, eye-level camera, very slow push-in.  
At the beginning, the character’s eyes look dull and lifeless.  
Over the next few seconds, a very faint glimmer gradually appears in the eyes.  
The change should be subtle and progressive, not sudden.  
Do not change the facial features or hairstyle.  
Preserve the exact identity and character design.

### 適合
- 情緒連續段落
- 崩潰 → 理解 → 恢復

---

## 範例 4：雙人對話不換位
### Prompt
Two characters remain in their original positions throughout the shot.  
Character A stays on the left foreground.  
Character B stays on the right background.  
Character A speaks and makes a small hand gesture.  
Character B only reacts with the eyes and a slight head movement.  
Do not swap their positions.  
Do not mix their faces, hairstyles, outfits, accessories, or body proportions.  
Preserve the original composition and camera angle.

### 適合
- 雙人劇情
- 前後景角色固定
- 避免人物互換

---

## 範例 5：參考影片角色替換
### Prompt
Use the reference video strictly for motion, timing, body movement, camera movement, framing, and environment.  
Replace only the designated person with the target character from the reference image.  
Keep the target character’s exact face, hairstyle, outfit, colors, accessories, and body proportions.  
Preserve the original motion rhythm and pose sequence from the reference video.  
Do not import the original person’s appearance.  
Do not add weapons or accessories that are not present in the target character reference.

### 適合
- 遊戲角色替換真人
- 參考影片重演
- 動作照抄、角色替換

---

## 範例 6：明顯震驚
### Prompt
Medium close-up, eye-level camera.  
The character suddenly freezes for a brief moment, then the eyes widen clearly, eyebrows lift, and the mouth opens slightly in genuine shock.  
The reaction should be obvious but not exaggerated into comedy.  
Use a subtle quick push-in at the moment of realization.  
Preserve the exact face and character design.

---

## 範例 7：崩潰
### Prompt
Medium close-up, slightly handheld camera.  
The character’s expression rapidly deteriorates from shock into emotional collapse.  
The shoulders drop, the breathing becomes visibly heavier, and the face shows clear frustration and disbelief.  
The motion is expressive but remains physically natural.  
Do not deform the face.  
Preserve the exact identity, hairstyle, outfit, and body proportions.

---

## 範例 8：拍手微笑
### Prompt
Medium shot, eye-level camera.  
The character gives a small genuine smile and begins clapping naturally.  
The hands move clearly into frame and complete several visible claps.  
The movement should be relaxed and readable, not too fast.  
Preserve the exact face, hairstyle, outfit, accessories, and body proportions.

---

## 範例 9：只動作、不碰物件
### Prompt
The character remains in place and only reacts through facial expression and subtle upper-body movement.  
The character does not walk toward the cabinet, does not touch the cabinet, does not open any drawer, and does not interact with any object in the environment.  
No supernatural effect.  
No additional props.  
Preserve the exact character appearance.

### 適合
當某一幕明確禁止走向、觸碰、打開物件時。

---

## 範例 10：台灣中文逐字台詞
### Prompt
The character speaks only the following dialogue in Traditional Chinese as spoken in Taiwan:

「[台詞]」

Do not translate the dialogue.  
Do not rewrite the dialogue.  
Do not add English speech.  
Do not add English subtitles.  
The spoken line must match the provided Chinese text exactly.

---

## 使用規則
1. 不整段照抄，要依鏡頭內容替換
2. 高風險角色先用頭幀
3. 單段只保留一個主要動作
4. 多角色要寫位置
5. 動作太小就補速度、幅度、起點、終點
